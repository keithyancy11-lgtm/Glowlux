Our Product Benefits and Expected Results

Skin-care products such as cleansers and moisturizers may help remove everyday dirt, add moisture and leave skin feeling cleaner, softer and more comfortable when used as directed. Perfumes are used to add fragrance. How long a scent lasts can depend on its formula, how it is applied, the weather and individual skin. Customers should follow the product label and stop using a product if irritation occurs.

Our Customers' Safety

Customers should read the ingredient list and directions, check for known sensitivities and try new skin products cautiously. Avoid applying products to irritated or broken skin. GlowLux only sells genuine products from reliable suppliers and does not promise the same results for every customer.
